1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Passport & ID Photo Maker Android mobile application ("the App"), developed and maintained by Basker ("the developer"). This document explains our data practices, distinguishing clearly between our local, private photo-editing features and the standardized technical data processed by third-party advertising, consent, and analytics software development kits (SDKs).

2. Photo Processing & Facial Landmark Guides (100% On-Device)

The core functionality of Passport & ID Photo Maker is to assist users in preparing compliant passport, visa, and identification photographs. Your photographs receive maximum privacy protection:

  • Local On-Device Execution & Storage: When you capture a photograph using your device camera or select an image from your gallery via the system Photo Picker, photo processing occurs locally on your physical device. The App utilizes device memory and app-private temporary storage where required (such as staging temporary camera captures in the application's internal cache). Unexported intermediate images are held within the application's private sandbox and are not stored in public device directories or uploaded to remote servers.
  • Facial Landmark Detection & Alignment: The App uses Google ML Kit Face Detection, executing locally on your device hardware, to detect facial contours and render visual alignment guides (e.g. chin-to-crown ratio and eye-level positioning markers) to assist in framing standard ID photos. Facial landmark coordinates are transient, held in temporary memory during editing and alignment, are not persisted to permanent storage or logged, and are discarded as transient objects upon conclusion of the editing session or when navigating away from the editor. The App does not use facial landmark information to identify or authenticate individuals.
  • Background Removal & Replacement: Subject segmentation and background color substitution are executed entirely on your device using Google Play Services ML Kit Subject Segmentation. At no point are your image cutouts uploaded to cloud servers.
  • Model Downloads vs. Photo Processing: Photo detection, facial alignment, and subject segmentation are processed locally on your device. Google Play Services may periodically download or update the machine-learning model files required for these features. These inbound model asset downloads are managed directly by Google Play Services and do not involve uploading, transmitting, or sharing your photographs or facial landmark data with Google or developer servers.
  • No Cloud Photo Processing: We do not operate external web servers, databases, or cloud processing pipelines for user images. The App does not transmit photographs to developer-operated servers.
  • Exporting & User-Initiated Photo Sharing: When you save or export a photo or print sheet, the resulting image is written to your device's local storage (e.g. your device gallery or documents folder). You may independently export, print, or share your completed photographs using Android system share features or other third-party applications (such as email, messaging apps, or cloud storage). Any subsequent sharing or transmission initiated by you through another application is independent of the App and outside the App's own photo-processing activity; the developer does not receive, view, or control photographs shared through external applications.

3. Third-Party Advertising: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

To support ongoing development and maintenance while keeping the application free to use, the App integrates the official Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen Android SDK (com.google.android.libraries.ads.mobile.sdk:ads-mobile-sdk:1.6.0).

Ad Placements & Frequency Capping

  • Banner Advertisements: Displayed exclusively at the bottom of the Home screen. Banners are never displayed inside photo selection, camera capture, cropping, editing, or export screens.
  • Post-Export Interstitial Advertisements: Full-screen advertisements may appear only at a natural transition after you have successfully saved or exported a photo. Interstitials are subject to frequency caps (at most once every 3 completed photo exports) and enforce a minimum 5-minute cooldown between impressions. Interstitials never interrupt photo saving or editing.
  • Local Frequency Cap Implementation: The App's photo export counter and cooldown timers are tracked strictly on your device using private local storage (SharedPreferences). The App does not use the Google Advertising ID or any external tracking identifier to enforce its local export frequency policy.

Information Processed by Google Mobile Ads

Depending on the device, operating system, consent status and advertising configuration, Google Mobile Ads may process device identifiers, IP addresses, general location information, technical device information, ad interactions and diagnostics to provide advertising services, measure performance and help prevent fraud.

Depending on your jurisdiction, consent choices, and operating system settings, advertisements served by Google may be personalized (tailored to your interests) or non-personalized (contextual). When personalized advertising is declined or unavailable, contextual or limited advertisements may still be served as permitted by applicable consent and platform policies. For comprehensive details regarding Google's advertising technologies, please review Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

5. Application Analytics: Google Firebase Analytics

The App incorporates Google Firebase Analytics to understand general usage trends and evaluate navigation flow performance.

Google services may process technical information and identifiers to provide analytics and advertising services. Google Analytics uses IP addresses at collection time for purposes such as determining approximate geographic location and data routing, and discards raw IP addresses after use; the developer does not receive or store users' raw IP addresses through analytics reports. Other Google services, including advertising services, may process IP addresses and other technical information under their respective privacy policies.

To protect your privacy, we have explicitly disabled Google Advertising ID collection for analytics in our configuration (google_analytics_adid_collection_enabled = false). Disabling Advertising ID collection prevents Firebase from using your advertising identifier for ad-tracking purposes; however, it does not disable all technical information processed by Firebase Analytics. Firebase Analytics automatically processes pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Firebase App Instance ID or installation ID) and general device and OS metadata to generate reporting views and statistics. The App also utilizes an internal privacy filter to ensure that photographs, filenames, face coordinates, and personal image data are strictly excluded from custom analytics events.

6. Device Permissions Used

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Requested only when you explicitly choose "Take Photo" to capture a live portrait. Captured camera frames are stored in app-private temporary storage and are never uploaded or streamed to external servers.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Enables network communication for advertising requests, consent information and messaging, analytics transmission, and other verified Google service functionality (such as ML model downloads managed by Google Play Services). The presence of network permission does not mean that your photos are transmitted over the Internet.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Included in the application manifest to support Google Mobile Ads functionality. Where available and permitted by applicable settings and consent choices, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may access or process advertising identifiers for advertising services, measurement and fraud prevention in accordance with Google's policies. Advertising ID collection for Firebase Analytics is separately disabled through the relevant manifest configuration. The App does not use this identifier for its own internal photo-processing logic or local export-frequency pacing.
  • Storage & Media Access (Android MediaStore & Storage Access Framework): When you export a photo or print sheet, files are saved directly into your device's public Pictures/PassportPhotoMaker or Documents/PassportPhotoMaker directories, or to a custom folder selected via the system file picker. The App does not read, inspect, or collect unrelated files or personal media from your device.
